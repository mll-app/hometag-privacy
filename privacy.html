隱私權政策與免責聲明 Privacy Policy & Disclaimer

「收納尋寶」是一款家庭物品盤點 App。這份文件說明這個 App 實際會、以及不會處理哪些資料，還有使用上的免責事項。

HomeTag is a household inventory app. This document explains exactly what data it does and doesn't handle, and the terms under which it's provided.

§1

關於「收納尋寶」About HomeTag

「收納尋寶」幫助家庭記錄家裡的物品：放在哪個房間、哪個儲藏空間、還剩多少、什麼時候該補貨。你可以手動輸入，也可以拍下收據，讓 AI 幫忙讀取品項、數量與價格。

HomeTag helps a household keep track of what it owns: which room, which storage space, how much is left, and when it's worth restocking. You can add items by hand, or photograph a receipt and let AI read out the items, quantities, and prices.

這個 App 刻意不做傳統的「帳號密碼」系統。你的資料不會存在我們的伺服器資料庫裡，而是留在你的裝置上，並透過端對端加密的方式在你自己的裝置之間同步。這個設計選擇也是這份政策存在的原因，因為我們能拿到的東西，本來就比一般 App 少。

Deliberately, this app has no traditional account/password system. Your data isn't stored in a server database we control; it lives on your device and syncs end-to-end between your own devices. That design choice is also why this document can say, plainly, that there's less for us to have access to in the first place.

§2

隱私權政策Privacy Policy

2.1　你的資料實際上在哪裡

2.1　Where Your Data Actually Lives

收納尋寶沒有使用者資料庫。下面每一項都只存在你的裝置上，並以端對端加密的方式在你自己的裝置之間同步。

HomeTag has no user database. Everything below lives only on your device and syncs end-to-end encrypted between your own devices.

物品盤點資料Inventory data
物品名稱、分類、數量、價格、房間／儲藏空間、加入與用完日期。
Item names, categories, quantities, prices, room/storage location, added and used-up dates.
物品照片Item photos
只存在拍照的那台裝置上，從不上傳到任何伺服器。
Stay only on the device that took them, never uploaded anywhere.
收據照片（掃描功能）Receipt photos (scan feature)
暫時傳送給 AI 供應商以讀取品項，處理後不留存。
Sent transiently to our AI provider to read items off the receipt; not retained afterward by us.
裝置識別碼Device token
隨機產生的代碼，只用來限制 AI 功能的每日使用次數，不是硬體序號。
A randomly generated code used only to rate-limit AI features, not a hardware identifier.
所在地區（選填）Location (optional)
你自己輸入的文字，例如「台北市大安區」，只有在你主動使用市價查詢時才會用到。
Free text you type yourself, e.g. a city or district, used only when you actively run a price check.
我們沒有做的事What we don't do
不會自動讀取 GPS 位置、不使用分析或廣告追蹤工具、不要求電子郵件或電話號碼。
No automatic GPS location, no analytics or ad-tracking SDKs, no email or phone number required to use the app.

2.2　資料如何儲存與同步

2.2　How Data Is Stored & Synced

你的物品盤點資料主要儲存在你的裝置上，並透過 Jazz Cloud（我們使用的同步服務）以端對端加密的方式在你自己的裝置間同步，即使沒有訂閱付費方案也一樣會同步；訂閱只決定你能否邀請其他家庭成員加入。Jazz Cloud 只會經手加密過的內容，無法讀取實際內容。

Your inventory data lives primarily on your device and syncs end-to-end between your own devices via Jazz Cloud, our sync provider. This happens regardless of subscription status; a subscription only controls whether you can invite other household members in. Jazz Cloud only ever handles encrypted data and cannot read its contents.

2.4　資料分享

2.4　Data Sharing

我們不會出售或用於廣告目的分享你的資料。你的物品盤點資料只會分享給你主動邀請加入同一個家庭的成員。

We don't sell your data or share it for advertising. Your inventory data is only ever shared with members you actively invite into the same household.

2.5　你的控制權

2.5　Your Control

  • 你可以隨時在「設定」中把整個盤點資料匯出成 TSV 檔案。
  • You can export your entire inventory as a TSV file from Settings at any time.
  • 刪除 App 會移除裝置上的本機資料。目前沒有帳號密碼系統，App 內也還沒有畫面可以查看或匯出救援密語，因此如果這是這個家唯一還連著的裝置，刪除後將無法重新連回同一份資料；若家裡還有其他裝置保留著資料，可以透過該裝置重新產生邀請連結。
  • Deleting the app removes local data on that device. There's no account/password system, and the app doesn't currently have a screen to view or export a recovery phrase, so if this was the only device still connected to this household, deleting it means there's no way back into that same data. If another device in the household still has it, you can generate a fresh invite link from there.

2.6　兒童隱私

2.6　Children's Privacy

這個 App 並非針對兒童設計，我們不會刻意收集兒童的個人資料。

This app isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them.

2.7　Changes to This Policy

若這份政策有實質變動，我們會更新本頁面上方的文件版本與生效日期。

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the document version and effective date at the top of this page.

For privacy questions, email us directly at hometag_app@icloud.com, or reach us through the developer contact listed on the App Store.
