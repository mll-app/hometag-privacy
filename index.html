收納尋寶

HomeTag

家裡的東西到底放在哪？收納尋寶用房間與儲藏空間幫你記住每一樣東西，拍張照就能讓 AI 幫你登記，資料端對端加密，只在你自己的裝置之間同步。

Where did you put that, again? HomeTag tracks everything your household owns by room and storage space, lets AI log items straight from a photo, and keeps it all end-to-end encrypted, synced only between your own devices.

📷

拍照即登記

Snap a photo, done

拍收據，AI 自動讀出品項、數量與價格；拍單一物品，AI 也能直接辨識是什麼、該放哪。

Photograph a receipt and AI reads out the items, quantities, and prices. Photograph a single object and AI identifies it directly.

🏠

依房間、儲藏空間整理

Organized by room & storage

每樣東西都對應到一個實際的房間與儲藏位置，還能記住上次放哪裡，下次自動幫你猜。

Every item maps to a real room and storage spot, and HomeTag remembers where things usually go, so it can suggest a place next time.

🔒

端對端加密同步

End-to-end encrypted sync

不需要帳號密碼。資料留在你的裝置上，只在你自己的裝置之間同步，我們看不到內容。

No account or password needed. Your data lives on your devices and syncs only between them, and we can't see the contents.

👨‍👩‍👧

全家一起維護

Share with your household

產生一個邀請連結，就能讓家人加入同一份盤點資料，一起記錄、一起找東西。

Generate an invite link so family members can join the same inventory, and everyone logs and finds things together.

⏰

用量與到期提醒

Usage & expiry reminders

追蹤快用完或快過期的東西，該補貨時不用臨時才想起來。

Track what's running low or coming up on its expiry, so restocking isn't a last-minute scramble.

🚫

沒有廣告、沒有追蹤

No ads, no tracking

不使用任何分析或廣告追蹤工具，App 裡也沒有廣告。

No analytics or ad-tracking SDKs of any kind, and no ads anywhere in the app.
